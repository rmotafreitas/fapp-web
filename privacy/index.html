Privacy notice

Last updated 12 November 2026.

Who this is for

Fapp is a personal, non-commercial app used only by its author, Ricardo Freitas, to view his own bank accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data is used

With the account holder's consent, given at the bank, Fapp reads account details, balances and transactions through Enable Banking Oy, a licensed account information service provider. The only data processed is the author's own.

Where it is stored

Data is stored only on the author's phone, in a local database. Credentials are kept in the phone's keychain. Nothing is sent to servers run by the author, and nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

This website

This site has no analytics, cookies or trackers. The callback page only passes the bank's response back to the app on the same device. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose own privacy statement applies to its hosting logs.

Your rights and contact

Consent can be withdrawn at any time at the bank or by disconnecting in the app, which can also delete the downloaded data. Contact: github.com/rmotafreitas.
